# Positioning Statements: Prompt Saver
*Skill: startup-positioning | Generated: 2026-04-02*

## Moore Positioning Statement

For **developers who write and reuse structured LLM prompts** who **waste time rewriting prompts they already perfected because they're scattered across chat history, Notion, and random files**, **Prompt Saver** is a **prompt library** that **saves every prompt with automatic versioning and instant search**. Unlike **enterprise prompt management platforms** (PromptLayer, Langfuse), our product **is free, requires no account, and works in seconds — not weeks of onboarding**.

## Neumeier Onliness Statement

Prompt Saver is the only **prompt library** that **versions every edit and searches across all your prompts instantly — for free, with zero setup**.

## Onliness Statement (Extended)

- **WHAT:** Prompt library for developers
- **HOW:** Automatic versioning + instant full-text search, zero setup, free
- **WHO:** Individual developers who write 20+ structured LLM prompts
- **WHERE:** Global, web-based
- **WHY:** Prompts are reusable assets worth organizing, not disposable text
- **WHEN:** 95% of developers now use AI weekly, creating a flood of prompts worth saving

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

"You know how you write a really good prompt — spend 20 minutes getting it perfect — and then three days later you can't find it? It's buried in your ChatGPT history or some Notion page. So you rewrite it from scratch. Prompt Saver fixes that. Save any prompt, it's automatically versioned, and you can find it by searching any word in it. It's free, no signup, works in your browser. Takes 10 seconds to save your first prompt."

## Tagline Candidates

| Tagline | Angle | Best For | Possible Misread |
|---------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Your prompts, saved and searchable. | Outcome — clarity | Homepage hero, social bios | Could sound generic — "saved" is passive |
| Never rewrite a prompt again. | Pain — the rewriting problem | Homepage hero, ads | Might imply auto-generation, not saving |
| The prompt library you wish you had. | Aspiration — fills a gap | Landing page, Product Hunt | Slightly presumptuous — "you wish" |
| Save it. Version it. Find it. | Action — three verbs | README, GitHub, technical audience | Sounds like a dev tool pitch, not user benefit |
| Stop losing your best prompts. | Pain — the scatter problem | Ads, social, email subject | Slightly negative framing |

**Recommended:** "Your prompts, saved and searchable." — clean, outcome-focused, no jargon.
